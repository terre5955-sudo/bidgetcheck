Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. Who we are

BidGapCheck is operated by Mark Mamedov, established in Poland, reachable at sigmovart@gmail.com. For data protection purposes this party is the data controller.

2. What we collect

  • Account data. Email address and a hashed password. We never store your password in readable form.
  • Session data. A session token in a cookie, so you stay signed in.
  • Uploaded documents. The tender PDFs you upload, and the reports generated from them.
  • Company profiles. Company information you choose to save, such as turnover, certifications and key personnel.
  • Technical data. IP address and request logs retained briefly for security and abuse prevention.
  • Payments. Card details are collected and stored by our payment processor, not by us. We receive only the customer identifier, subscription identifier, and payment status.

3. Why we process it, and on what basis

  • To provide the service and operate your account: performance of a contract.
  • To take payment and keep records: legal obligation and legitimate interest.
  • To prevent abuse and secure the service: legitimate interest.
  • To email you about your account or, if you opt in, product changes: consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

We do not use your documents for advertising. We do not sell personal data. We do not use uploaded tender documents to train machine learning models.

4. Who we share it with

  • Lemon Squeezy, our payment processor and "Merchant of Record". They collect the customer's payment and charge and remit any applicable sales tax or VAT, then pay us net. They act as an independent controller for that processing.
  • We do not send marketing email. The service sends no email today; password reset and receipt delivery are handled by our payment processor. If transactional email is added, the provider will be named here before it is used.
  • Render, our hosting provider, which runs the application and database on our behalf under contract.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell data to advertisers, data brokers, or model providers.

5. International transfers

Our providers are located in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside your country, we rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses published by the European Commission.

6. How long we keep it

  • Event and security logs, which include your IP address: 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Uploaded documents and generated reports: until you delete them or your account.
  • Account data: for the life of the account.
  • Payment and invoice records: as long as tax law requires.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to complain to a supervisory authority.

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from the application. This removes your profile, your saved company profiles, your uploaded documents and your reports. For anything else, use the contact address in section 1. We respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

We use one essential cookie, bgc_session, which keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary and does not require consent under most privacy regimes. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

9. Security

Passwords are hashed with a memory-hard function. Session tokens are stored hashed-side and expire. Uploaded documents are stored per account and are not served to other users. Access to production systems is limited. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the relevant authority within the legally required timeframe.

10. Children

The service is a business tool and is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

We will update this policy when our processing changes. Material changes will be announced in the product or by email.
