Why there is no "unlimited" plan

Because you should not have to pay for a product you use twice a year, and I should not have to price for a user I never get. If you bid more than 150 documents a month, email and we will sort it out.

What changes on Pro

The verdict is the main difference. The free tier runs on deterministic pattern matching, which finds deadlines, disqualifying clauses, required documents and thresholds reliably. Pro adds a language model pass that reads requirement structure the patterns cannot see, then checks your saved company profile against every mandatory line and returns a bid or no-bid verdict with named blockers.

Refunds

Seven days, no questions, on both plans. Email the address you signed up with. Subscription cancels at the end of the paid period, not immediately, so you keep access until you paid up to.

Scanned PDFs

Text-based PDFs only. A tender scanned from paper has no text layer and cannot be parsed. This is a hard limitation, not a temporary one, and it is not covered by your plan.
