# Outreach at zero budget

The rule: **never pitch a stranger with the product. Sell the document you already read.**

## Channel 1 - award notice mining (best first move)

Public tender portals publish award notices naming the company that won.

- EU: TED (ted.europa.eu), Find a Tender (find-tender.service.gov.uk), national portals
- US: SAM.gov, state procurement portals
- World Bank and EBRD publish awards for funded projects

You get a company name that has already proven it bids in that category. That is a warm
lead with a public reason to contact them, and you can reference a real contract.

Template:

> Hi {Name} - saw {Company} won {tender title} ({portal}, {reference}).
> I run one-page tender risk reviews. You read the document and tell me the bidding
> rules, I come back within 24 hours with every disqualifying clause and its page
> number. EUR 49, or free if you just want the deadline and top 5 risks.
> Want me to look at anything live right now?

## Channel 2 - LinkedIn, vertical only

Pick **one** vertical for 30 days. Not "IT", but "IT vendors bidding on UK NHS trust
supplier frameworks" or "civil subcontractors bidding on Irish county council works".
Vertical focus is what makes a cold message land.

Search titles: `Bid Manager`, `Tender Manager`, `Procurement Specialist`, `Pre-Sales`,
`Commercial Manager`, `Contracts Officer`, `Founder` (small firms often have no
procurement role at all).

Connect note, under 300 characters, no link, no pitch:

> Not pitching. I saw your company bid on public work - what is the worst part of that
> for you, reading 200 pages of tender documentation?

Only message after they accept, or after a genuine public interaction.

## Channel 3 - communities, value only

r/smallbusiness, r/contractors, r/FreelanceForhire, procurement-specific groups, local
business associations, APMP chapters.

Never paste a sales link. Post the actual finding:

> Read a 180-page tender this week. Three clauses that silently disqualify, all in the
> small print on page 4 of the instructions section: [details]. The document says the
> submission is "rejected as non-responsive", which sounds final and is buried in a
> footnote. Nobody reads footnotes. Here are the three sentences verbatim.

That post is your entire marketing. Repeat it weekly with a different document.

## Channel 4 - procurement consultants

Consultants bill EUR 1k-5k per tender. They are the highest-margin customer you can have
and they actively want this because it removes their boring first pass.

> I built a review pass that extracts every mandatory clause with page numbers and
> verifies each quote against the source PDF. It takes about a minute on a 200-page
> document. If you want it as a white-label PDF you can send under your own name, the
> first ten are free.

## The daily number

5-10 new contacts per day, 2 follow-ups, 1 public post per week.
At 5 hours a week that is roughly one working session.

Track in a plain text file, one line per contact:
`date | company | tender | channel | stage | next action date`
A spreadsheet at zero revenue is overhead. Text is fine.

## Objections and honest answers

**"We already read it."**
Then you know the cost. Ask: "Who reads it, and how long does it take?"
If they answer "three people, four hours each", that is twelve hours. EUR 49 for twelve
hours is arithmetic, not a sale.

**"Send it over and we'll look."**
"Sending a contract for tender work over email is how you leak an RFP. It goes to my
local machine, not a server. Here is the tool running, you can see it yourself."

**"Our procurement person handles it."**
"Then forward this to them." Nine times in ten they do not.

**"Too expensive."**
Then do not bid it. Say so: "If the contract is under EUR 10k, honestly, do not pay for
this and do not bid either. Above EUR 50k, EUR 49 is nothing against being thrown out
in week three."

## What not to do

- Do not build a landing page before you have 3 conversations.
- Do not run ads. Zero budget means zero ads.
- Do not chase enterprise. One enterprise sales cycle will eat your 350 hours.
- Do not offer a discount below EUR 39. Price is how you qualify who is serious.
