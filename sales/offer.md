# The offer

## What you sell

**Tender Risk Review** - a same-day document review that tells a bidding company
whether a tender is worth pursuing and what will disqualify them.

Not "AI proposal writer". Not "platform". One document in, one decision out.

## The pitch in one sentence

> Before you spend 40 hours writing this proposal, find out whether one page of the
> tender disqualifies you.

## Why this can be sold at low price

The competitor set charges far more for a narrower job:

| Vendor | Price | Minimum commitment |
|---|---|---|
| AutoRFP.ai | $899-1299/mo | 24 projects/year |
| RFP Matrix | $150-500/mo | subscription |
| DeepRFP | $89/user/mo | subscription |
| Lucius | EUR 149 per tender | per tender |
| RFP Matrix one-off | $100 | none |
| TenderAI add-on | $29/audit | on top of $199/mo |

Their minimum price excludes the buyer who matters most to you: the IT subcontractor or
consultancy bidding EUR 30k-300k jobs, one to three times a quarter. That person loses
the entire contract value to a missing certificate page. A EUR 49 review is a 0.02-0.15%
insurance premium on the contract.

## Price ladder

**Free tier (lead magnet)** - submit the tender, get back free:
- the submission deadline with page number
- the top 5 disqualifying clauses with page numbers
- page count and document type

Deliberately give away the cheapest thing you can produce. It proves you actually read
the document, which is the entire objection you have.

**EUR 49 - Full Risk Review** (single document, paid per report)
- every mandatory requirement, severity-rated, page-cited
- every document that must be submitted
- compliance checkpoints: insurance, turnover, experience, personnel, bond, format
- monetary thresholds and who they bind
- one-page summary your bid manager can read in 60 seconds

**EUR 199 - Review + submission checklist** (adds)
- a per-requirement checklist mapping your company profile against the tender
- bid / no-bid verdict with the blockers named
- priced as a bundle, sold only to people who already saw the free tier

**EUR 25-60/mo - Keep It Alive** (only after you have 10 paying customers)
- re-run the review when an addendum (Addendum 2, Q&A clarifications) lands
- this is the subscription wedge, and it only works once the client trusts the one-off

## What you must not promise

- Never promise "you will win the bid".
- Never give legal advice on whether a clause is enforceable.
- Always state that every line carries a page number and was verified against the source.
- Say "assists a human decision" in every deliverable. It is in the report footer already.

## The moat, stated honestly

You have no moat on the AI. Claude, GPT and open-source models do the same extraction.
You have three real advantages and they compound:

1. **Verified citations.** Every sentence in your report exists in the client's PDF,
   character for character. Generic AI hallucinates clause text and a bid manager catches
   it instantly. This is why the check exists in the code.
2. **A profile-per-customer memory.** When company X comes back with tender number two,
   you already know their turnover, certifications and turnover history. That is a
   switching cost, and it is free to build.
3. **Anonymised outcome data.** After 50 reviews you know which clauses actually
   disqualify people in which country. Nobody can buy that. It takes years, and it is
   the only thing here that is genuinely scarce.

## Kill criteria

Kill or pivot the offer if, after 60 targeted conversations and 15 delivered reviews:

- fewer than 3 people pay when offered EUR 49 for a live tender, or
- every buyer says "we already read it carefully", or
- buyers ask for the tool itself rather than the answer.

If all three hold, the pain is not real. Move on. Do not build the product to justify the
research.
